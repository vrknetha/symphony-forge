# Shorten reviews and carry plan requirements through to close

5 parts · Risks: none one-way · New moving parts: none

## What changes for you

- When you accept a stopped plan read, each kept note now says one of two things: "accepted risk" (nobody builds anything for it) or "builder must prove" (it names the part and a check anyone can observe). A must-prove check reaches that part's builder in its brief and the code review's instructions, and close checks it against the named test or a real run of the flow. A check that can only be proven after merge keeps its owner and its check in the pull request. Notes kept before this release stay accepted risks. This replaces the hand workaround of moving each note into the Done-when details. (Source: GitHub issue 62; your decision of 28 Nov 2026)
- A review finding that was dismissed stays dismissed when the reviewer repeats it in new words, as long as the lines it cites have not changed. Changed lines or new evidence bring it back. (Source: your decision of 28 Nov 2026)
- A fix gets one full code review and a story part gets up to two. The second round checks the earlier findings and admits only a new serious problem (P0) that happens in normal use. At the limit, every other open finding becomes a named follow-up and Forge asks you nothing. An open P0, or a requirement you approved that is still unmet, stops and asks you: one more round, split, or accept where accepting is allowed. The coordinator cannot dismiss it. The pinned Autoreview stays the review engine. (Source: your decision of 28 Nov 2026)
- Plan reads hold less. A note labelled Advisory never holds a plan. A note at Gain 3 and Harm 3, or a rare case below Harm 4, no longer holds. From round two only Harm 4 or 5, or a note that says an earlier note is not fixed, holds. Answering a note may only narrow the plan: a new table, flow or question for a person is deferred or comes to you. At the read limit the open notes go to the leaf parts as must-prove checks and the plan goes on to approval, with no question to you. Code review keeps today's bar. (Source: your decision of 28 Nov 2026)
- The reviewer is always a different model family than the builder: Terra max reviews work built by Claude or Antigravity. Codex-built work keeps Terra max until Claude passes the recorded cases; that is a test to run later, not a switch now. The first cold reader is a different family than the plan's author and stays for the repairs. (Source: your decision of 28 Nov 2026)
- What each repo gets: a new repo gets all of this from its first read, build and review. An existing repo gets it when it upgrades to the release that has this story. Its configured models, recorded owner choices and open stops are kept: a stop that was open before the upgrade still waits for your answer, and a kept note keeps its meaning. No command and no setting is added. (Source: your decision of 28 Nov 2026)

Design: none (no screen changes)

## Why

Forge's records (measured by Astra on 28 Nov 2026 from Forge's own records, cached `origin/main` plus local branches) show review rounds that mostly find less each time, and a gap between what the owner decides and what the builder is told.

- Median model input where it was recorded: 542k per fix review, 494k per part review, 1.94M per plan read. These are the baselines for the release after this one.
- 226 of 1,675 fix findings and 108 of 936 part findings repeat an earlier title and file. In the best checked sample, 17 of 43 blockers (40%) were real bugs and 2 asked for proof that exists only after merge. The rest are not shown to be false.
- Of 443 advisory plan notes, today's bar would hold 127, 124 of them after round one. In the owner's eight long client reads, 235 of 277 notes held, and about 17 of 29 later blockers in the longest read came from the earlier amendments.
- Six later fixes describe problems the coordinator had set aside. One saved instruction assigns seven accepted notes to builders; nothing carries them there (issue 62).
- Across ten replayed fixes, Terra at high effort found 8 of 13 confirmed defects, and at max effort 11 of 13. The 83% input saving at high effort failed the owner's quality rule, so reviews stay on max.

## Done when

1. **A cold-read note's decision is either "accepted risk" or "builder must prove". A must-prove note names the part and an observable check, shows in that part's builder brief and in both review prompts, and close checks it against the named test or real-flow result. A check that can only be proven after merge keeps its owner and check in the pull request. Notes kept before this release stay accepted risks.**
2. **A dismissal stays with the same defect when the reviewer words it differently and the cited lines are unchanged; changed lines or new evidence reopen it.**
3. **A fix gets one full code review and a story part up to two. Round two checks the earlier findings and admits only a new P0 that happens in normal use. At the limit, ordinary leftovers become named follow-ups; an open P0 or an unmet owner-approved requirement stops for the owner, and the coordinator cannot dismiss it. The pinned Autoreview still runs one pass per round.**
4. **A plan note labelled Advisory never holds a read; Gain 3 with Harm 3, and a rare case below Harm 4, no longer hold; from round two only Harm 4 or 5, or a note that says an earlier one is not fixed, holds; and at the read limit the open notes become must-prove checks for the builders and the plan goes to approval. Code review keeps its bar.**
5. **Each code review round comes from a different model family than the builder (Terra max for Claude and Antigravity work; Codex-built work keeps Terra max until Claude passes the recorded cases), and the first cold reader comes from a different family than the plan's author and stays for repairs.**
6. **A new repo gets all of this at setup and an existing repo on upgrade, keeping its configured models, recorded owner choices and open stops.**

## Risks

Risks: none one-way. Each part reverts with its pull request.

Accepted limits:
- A real but ordinary defect (Gain 4, or Gain 3 with Harm 3) found in a fix's one full review becomes a named follow-up instead of a repair. A P0 and an unmet approved requirement are still repaired first.
- A plan note at Harm 3 raised new in round two no longer stops the plan. It reaches the builders only if the reader keeps it as a must-prove note, and the code review still checks the code.
- At the read limit nobody chooses a split: open notes go to the builders as must-prove checks even when a separate story would fit better. A spec or decision has no builders, so its open notes at the limit stay accepted risks.
- Codex-built work is still reviewed by Codex (Terra max) until Claude passes the recorded cases, so that one pairing stays same-family on purpose.
- A repo with one tool (`tools = claude` or `tools = codex`) has no second family and keeps reviewing with its tool. An Antigravity coordinator is not detected, so the plan author's family is read only from a Claude Code or Codex session, and a coordinator of another family who takes over a plan and starts its first read counts as its author.
- A must-prove check is judged by the reviewer. A wrong "unmet" holds the part until a person accepts it where accepting is allowed.
